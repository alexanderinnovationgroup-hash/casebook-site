Privacy Policy — Casebook

Last updated: November 2025

The short version: Casebook stores everything on your own device. Your case files, evidence, notes, and account credentials never leave your computer through our software. We cannot see them because they are never sent to us.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into Casebook — documents, screenshots, messages, photos, recordings, transcripts, timelines, notes, and reports — is stored locally on your device and nowhere else. Casebook does not include analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting uploads, advertising SDKs, or third-party trackers.

What leaves your device (and when)

  • Activation and updates — only when you ask. Entering a license key and checking for an update happen only when you click the button. These requests contain no case data.
  • Payment. If you subscribe, payment is processed by our payment provider (listed at checkout). They receive your payment details under their own privacy policy; we receive only a confirmation and an email address so we can send your license key.
  • Optional timestamping. If you choose to anchor a record with OpenTimestamps, a cryptographic hash (a fingerprint — not the file) of your document is sent to public timestamp calendars. The document itself is never sent.
  • Your own exports. Any reports or bundles you choose to share with a lawyer or anyone else travel however you send them — we are not involved.

Your credentials

Your Casebook sign-in name and password live only on your device as a salted hash. They are not transmitted anywhere and cannot be recovered by us — if you forget the password, reinstalling the app resets the lock (your case folders are not touched).

Data we hold

On our side, the only records are: your email address (if you purchased or contacted us), your license key status, and correspondence you send us.

Children's information

Casebook is a tool used by adults to organize their own records. Information about children stored in the app remains on the user's device, governed by this same local-only design.
