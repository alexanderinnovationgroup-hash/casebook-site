# Casebook — Launch & Outreach Plan

Audience: self-represented parents in family court. They search in panic at 2am
and trust people, not ads. Channels ranked by expected payoff:

## 1. Content / SEO (primary channel — free, compounds)

Publish genuinely useful guides on the site. Each = one page under `site/guides/`
linked from index.html. Reuse `resources/selfrep-guide.json` content — it's
already written, just needs web formatting:

- "How to organize text messages for family court"
- "Chain of custody for digital evidence, explained plainly"
- "What to bring to a custody hearing (a checklist that isn't legal advice)"
- "Screenshots as evidence: how to keep them credible"
- "Preparing your records before meeting a lawyer — saves real money"

Tone rules: "organize your records", never "win your case". Same honesty model
as the product.

## 2. Court self-help ecosystem (free)

Every courthouse has a family-law facilitator / self-help office; legal aid
clinics and law librarians maintain referral lists and see the "shoebox of
printouts" problem daily. Script: one paragraph email + the download link + a
one-page printable they can hand out. Target 10 offices first; ask what would
make them comfortable recommending it (usually: honest "not legal advice"
framing — which we already have).

## 3. DV & family service orgs (free)

Shelters and survivor advocates literally tell clients "document everything" —
Casebook is the tool for exactly that. Same for mediators, paralegals, and
guardians ad litem who see clients arrive disorganized.

## 4. Communities (careful — no spam)

r/Custody, r/SingleParents, state family-law subs, custody Facebook groups.
Direct self-promo gets banned — answer questions helpfully, use promo threads
where they exist, link only when someone asks for a tool.

## 5. The product markets itself

The lawyer-handoff bundle is the best ad — a lawyer receives a clean package
and asks "what made this?" Make sure the bundle footer names the product
(already does: check HANDOFF-CHECKLIST.txt / bundle footer).

## Realistic first week

1. Site live ✔ (done)
2. Publish 3–5 guides (extract from selfrep-guide.json)
3. Post where allowed + email 10 legal aid offices
4. Measure: download counts on the release assets, not impressions
